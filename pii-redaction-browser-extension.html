A PII redaction extension for AI chats: what runs on your device

A browser extension that redacts personal data before it reaches ChatGPT has to read what you type. That makes it worth asking hard questions before you install one, including ours. Here are the questions, and Redaxa's answers, each one checkable.

Four questions for any PII redaction extension

  1. Which sites can it read? Chrome lists an extension's site access on its details page. A redaction tool for AI chats needs the AI sites, not every page you open.
  2. Where does detection run? In your browser, or on the developer's server? If on a server, is the text stored or passed to another AI?
  3. Does it change your text silently? Automatic rewriting can break a prompt without you noticing. You should see what was found and decide.
  4. What does it actually detect, and what does it miss? A list of supported data types is a start; a list of known misses is better.

What it detects, and what it misses

The extension uses the same engine as the web check, the Windows app and the repository check. In one pass it looks for:

  • Personal data: email addresses, phone numbers, names after a greeting, English-format street addresses, IPv4 addresses, US Social Security numbers, UK National Insurance numbers and Italian fiscal codes.
  • Financial data: card numbers (Luhn-checked), IBANs (mod-97-checked) and crypto wallet addresses.
  • Credentials: API keys and tokens from more than a dozen providers, passwords, private keys and secrets in configuration. See how to stop API keys leaking into AI prompts.

Known limits, stated on the detector pages with real examples: a name is detected only right after a greeting such as "Hi" or "Dear"; addresses are recognised only in the English house-number-first format; the phone rule also matches some order and invoice numbers. For client and project names, use protected terms. The full list is in the detection reference.

FAQ

Can the extension read every website I visit?

No. Its content script is declared only for chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com and www.perplexity.ai, and its only permissions are storage and clipboardWrite. You can confirm this in the extension's details page in Chrome.

Does it change my message without asking?

No. When it finds something, it shows a notice before the message is sent and you choose: replace the text with the redacted version, send anyway, or close the notice and edit. Business admins can set a policy that blocks sending for a category.

Which languages does it support?

The extension's interface is available in English, Italian, Spanish, French and German. Detection patterns are the same for every language; the national identifiers it knows are US Social Security numbers, UK National Insurance numbers and Italian fiscal codes.

What does it cost?

Free checks in the extension are unlimited and need no account. Protected terms and team policies come with Pro or Business.
