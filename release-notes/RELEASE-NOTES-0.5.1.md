Redaxa 0.5.1 starts everyone in the light Paper theme unless they picked another one, including people whose earlier version saved the old default for them.

- Paper is the starting theme everywhere: the app, the website, its guides and its policy pages.
- If an earlier version saved Petrol & Copper without you choosing it, Redaxa now opens in Paper. A theme you pick in Settings is remembered as your choice; if you had picked Petrol & Copper yourself, choose it once more there.
- Sign-in and account dialogs on the website follow the theme as well.
- The window opens in Paper, with no dark frame while the app loads.

Windows installers require verified Aurelio Avila publisher signatures, trusted timestamps and compatible updater signatures before publication.
