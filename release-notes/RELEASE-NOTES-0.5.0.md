Redaxa 0.5.0 redesigns the app: one sidebar with a page for every task, a full Plans & pricing page, and a light Paper theme for anyone who has not chosen one.

- Check text, Repository check, Activity, Protected terms, Settings, Account & team and Plans & pricing each have their own page, reached from a sidebar that keeps your plan and account at the bottom.
- Check results are grouped by severity, from critical to low, and every value found is highlighted in your text.
- Plans & pricing is now a full page with your current plan, a side-by-side comparison, what leaves your device, and answers to common questions.
- Paper is the new default theme and Ink & Copper its dark counterpart. A theme you already picked stays as it is.
- Sign-in, account and team dialogs follow your theme, with readable fields in every palette.
- Pages switch as soon as you click, and the window opens at 1280 × 820.
