Redaxa 0.6.3 stays in the notification area when you close its window, the same way PC Tweaker does.

- Closing the window keeps Redaxa in the notification area. Click its icon to reopen it, or right-click it for Open Redaxa and Exit Redaxa.
- Turn off Keep running in the background from the icon's menu if you prefer closing the window to quit. Your choice is saved.
- Opening Redaxa, or a redaxa:// link, while it is already running brings its window back. Reopening it also checks for updates.
- The icon's menu and the notice shown the first time you close the window are in the language chosen in Settings.

Windows installers require verified Aurelio Avila publisher signatures, trusted timestamps and compatible updater signatures before publication.
