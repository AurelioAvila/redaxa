Redaxa 0.4.8 gives free accounts daily checks and recognises more kinds of tokens.

- Signed-in accounts without a plan get 5 free prompt checks a day, counted per account, before a plan is needed.
- Detects npm, PyPI, GitLab and Hugging Face access tokens.
- Pasting into the app works again where Windows clipboard access was blocked.
- Signing out on this PC no longer signs you out on your other devices.
- After checkout, the browser returns to redaxa.getcertsprint.com.

Windows installers require verified Aurelio Avila publisher signatures, trusted timestamps and compatible updater signatures before publication. Repository matches remain review candidates; no detector can guarantee zero false positives or prove that a credential is active.
