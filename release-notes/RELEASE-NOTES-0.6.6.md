Redaxa 0.6.6 keeps the free trial button in the sidebar tidy in every language and shows it on narrow windows too.

- The arrow of "Start your 3-day free trial" no longer drops onto a line of its own; the button's text now breaks evenly in English, Italian, Spanish, French and German.
- On phones and very narrow windows the free trial offer now stays in the top strip, so it is just as easy to find as on a wide screen.
