Redaxa 0.6.0 installs for everyone on the computer, in Program Files\Redaxa as Redaxa.exe, and setup now ends by offering to start Redaxa and to put a shortcut on the desktop. Updating moves your current installation there by itself; settings, history and sign-in stay as they are.

- Setup installs Redaxa for all users in `C:\Program Files\Redaxa`, and the program is `Redaxa.exe`. Windows asks for administrator permission when you install or update.
- The last page of setup offers "Run Redaxa" and "Create desktop shortcut", both ticked. The Start menu entry is created as before, and uninstalling removes the program, the Start menu entry and the desktop shortcut; your settings stay unless you choose to delete the app data.
- Updating from 0.5.5 or earlier moves the installation from your user folder (`%LocalAppData%\Redaxa`) to Program Files: the old copy, its entry in Installed apps and its Start menu shortcut are removed, and a desktop shortcut or taskbar pin you had opens the new program. Settings, check history and the saved sign-in are kept, because they are stored apart from the program.
- Plans & pricing reads its prices from the payment provider, and your account shows when a payment did not go through, with a button to update the payment method.

Windows installers require verified Aurelio Avila publisher signatures, trusted timestamps and compatible updater signatures before publication.
