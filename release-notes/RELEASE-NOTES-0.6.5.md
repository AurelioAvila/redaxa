Redaxa 0.6.5 makes the 3-day free trial easy to find and fixes the plan labels on Plans & pricing.

- Plans & pricing and the sidebar now offer "Start your 3-day free trial" to accounts that can still start one, show "Trial — X days left" while it runs, and say plainly when it has already been used.
- Every plan below the one you hold now reads "Included in your plan", so a Business account sees Pro as included instead of a blank card.

Windows installers require verified Aurelio Avila publisher signatures, trusted timestamps and compatible updater signatures before publication.
