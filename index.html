Questions

          

            
How many free checks do I get?
Visitors can try up to 5 prompt checks per day on the site, shared across their network; the Chrome extension's free checks have no daily limit. An active plan removes the daily limit on the site and in the Windows app.

            
How does the 7-day trial work?
Eligible new subscribers start with a 7-day trial. A card is required; cancel before the trial ends to avoid a charge.

            
Can I cancel or change my plan?
Yes. Your account opens the billing portal, where you can update your payment method, download invoices or cancel renewal whenever you need to.

            
What does Business add?
Everything in Pro, plus shared protected terms, category policies and metadata-only activity for your team. Choose one to three users.

            
Is the text I check stored?
No. Checks on the web, in the Windows app and with a plan are analyzed and discarded, and Redaxa keeps metadata only. Free checks in the Chrome extension never leave your device.

            
Will Redaxa find everything?
No tool can promise that. Detection can miss sensitive information or flag harmless text, so review every result before you share it.
