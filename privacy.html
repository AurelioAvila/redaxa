Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Redaxa is operated by Aurelio Avila, an individual based in Italy ("we", "us"). This page explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it. If anything here is unclear, email redaxa@getcertsprint.com.

The short version

The text you paste into the scanner is used only to compute the result you see. It is never written to a database, a log file, an analytics tool, or a backup. We collect account and billing details needed to run a subscription (email, optional first name and billing status), a record of each signed-in check processed on our servers that never includes the text, and the anonymous website action totals described below. We do not use advertising trackers or sell this data. Older accounts may retain profile details supplied before this change.

Sensitive data the scanner may process

Redaxa's whole purpose is to catch sensitive content before it leaves your browser, so the text you scan can legitimately contain any of the following:

  • Personally identifiable information — names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses.
  • Financial and payment information — credit/debit card numbers, IBANs and bank account numbers, cryptocurrency wallet addresses.
  • Authentication information — passwords, API keys, JWTs, private keys, security-question answers.

This content is processed only to detect and flag it to you. When a check runs on our servers (in the web app, the desktop app, or the browser extension with an active plan), the text is transmitted over an encrypted connection, used solely to compute the scan result shown to you, and is never written to a database, a log file, an analytics tool, or a backup. Free checks in the browser extension run entirely in your browser and the text is not sent to us.

Browser extension

The Redaxa browser extension runs only on the AI chat sites it supports (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity). It reads the text in the chat box when you click "Check" and, unless you turn off "Check automatically before sending" in the extension, when you press Enter or the send button. Without an active plan, the check runs entirely in your browser and the text is not sent anywhere. With an active Pro or Business plan, the text is sent to Redaxa so your workspace's protected terms and policies apply, as described above, together with the name of the AI site, which appears in your activity history. The extension does not read any other page content and does not track your browsing. Your sign-in tokens are kept in the extension's own storage, which the websites it runs on cannot read.

What we don't do

  • We don't use ad pixels or third-party trackers. The only measurement is the cookie-free counting described under Website measurement.
  • We don't sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We don't use your prompt content to train any model or for any purpose beyond showing you the scan result.

Who we share data with

Only the infrastructure providers required to run the service, each acting as a processor on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication and account database (EU region).
  • Stripe — payment processing. Card details are collected and stored by Stripe directly; we never handle them.
  • Vercel — application hosting.
  • Resend — delivery of account and subscription emails.

When you check a public GitHub repository, Redaxa downloads it from GitHub and sends the names and versions of its dependencies to OSV.dev to look up known vulnerabilities. Nothing about you is included in either request.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is active. Prompt text is never stored in the first place — there's nothing to retain or delete. To delete your account and associated billing/account data, email redaxa@getcertsprint.com; we'll confirm once it's done.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact redaxa@getcertsprint.com to exercise any of these. If you're in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Age

Redaxa is not directed at children. You must be at least 16 years old, or the age of digital consent in your country if higher, to create an account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where required, notify you by email.

Website measurement

We count website visits and predefined actions as daily totals. The measurement database stores only day, product, action and count: no prompt text, detected values, email addresses, account identifiers or IP addresses. Requests go to our Cloudflare service at redexa.getcertsprint.com. Page views on this site are also counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not fingerprint visitors. No tracking cookies or persistent visitor identifiers are set. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected. Counts are not unique users. Hosting providers process network information to deliver requests.
