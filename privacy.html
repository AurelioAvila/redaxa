Privacy policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Redaxa is operated by Aurelio Avila, an individual based in Italy ("we", "us"). This page explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it. If anything here is unclear, email aurelio_11@outlook.it.

The short version

The text you paste into the scanner is used only to compute the result you see. It is never written to a database, a log file, an analytics tool, or a backup. We collect the account details needed to run a subscription (email, name, date of birth, billing status) and nothing more — no ad trackers, no analytics scripts, no data sold to anyone.

Sensitive data the scanner may process

Redaxa's whole purpose is to catch sensitive content before it leaves your browser, so the text you scan can legitimately contain any of the following:

  • Personally identifiable information — names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses.
  • Financial and payment information — credit/debit card numbers, IBANs and bank account numbers, cryptocurrency wallet addresses.
  • Authentication information — passwords, API keys, JWTs, private keys, security-question answers.

This content is processed only to detect and flag it to you. It is transmitted over an encrypted connection, used solely to compute the scan result shown in your browser, and is never written to a database, a log file, an analytics tool, or a backup — on the web app, the browser extension, or the desktop app.

Browser extension

The Redaxa browser extension reads the text box of the AI chat site you're on (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity) only when you click "Check", to send it for scanning as described above. It does not read any other page content, does not run on any other site, and does not track your browsing. Your sign-in tokens are stored in the extension's private local storage, inaccessible to the web pages it runs on.

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics, ad pixels, or third-party trackers on this site.
  • We don't sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We don't use your prompt content to train any model or for any purpose beyond showing you the scan result.

Who we share data with

Only the infrastructure providers required to run the service, each acting as a processor on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication and account database (EU region).
  • Stripe — payment processing. Card details are collected and stored by Stripe directly; we never handle them.
  • Vercel — application hosting.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is active. Prompt text is never stored in the first place — there's nothing to retain or delete. To delete your account and associated billing/account data, email aurelio_11@outlook.it; we'll confirm once it's done.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact aurelio_11@outlook.it to exercise any of these. If you're in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Age

Redaxa is not directed at children. You must be at least 16 years old, or the age of digital consent in your country if higher, to create an account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where required, notify you by email.
