Redaxa 0.4.9 refreshes the Windows app and makes the window easier to handle.

- Drag the window from anywhere on the title bar, and double-click it to maximise or restore.
- Redaxa reopens where you left it, at the same size.
- A cleaner workspace: the prompt and its results share one panel, the sidebar keeps the same icons on every page, and dividers and numbers line up.
- Repository checks raise fewer false alarms on public projects: published demo keys, placeholders and test fixtures are reported as low.
- Accounts without a plan go straight to their 5 free daily checks instead of the sign-up dialog.

Windows installers require verified Aurelio Avila publisher signatures, trusted timestamps and compatible updater signatures before publication. Repository matches remain review candidates; no detector can guarantee zero false positives or prove that a credential is active.
